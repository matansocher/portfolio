# Product Designer & UX Researcher who designs with evidence.

_About Dekel_

I help teams build products people actually understand and enjoy using. For the past several years I have partnered with startups, enterprises, and public-sector teams to turn messy problems into clear, human-centered experiences.

## Design is a way of thinking, not a coat of paint.

I believe the best products come from teams that stay close to their users and make decisions out in the open. My job is to bring that clarity: to ask the right questions, run the research that answers them, and translate what we learn into interfaces that feel effortless. I care about the small details, honest states, forgiving flows, and plain language, because that is where trust is built.

## How I can help

### UX Research

Interviews, usability testing, and behavioral analysis that turn assumptions into decisions the whole team trusts.

### Product Design

End-to-end flows and interfaces, from first wireframe to polished, developer-ready design.

### Design Systems

Scalable component libraries and design tokens that keep fast-moving teams consistent.

### Product Strategy

Aligning design work with real business goals so every screen moves a metric that matters.

## My approach

1. **Understand** — I start with people and problems, not screens. Research first, opinions second.
2. **Define** — I frame the real problem and the decision we are trying to make before designing anything.
3. **Design** — Rapid, testable design that I put in front of users early and often.
4. **Deliver** — Clear, well-documented handoff and close partnership with engineering through launch.
