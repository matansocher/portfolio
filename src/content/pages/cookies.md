# Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This site uses browser storage for preferences and Google Analytics cookies. The cookie notice is informational and dismissing it does not stop analytics.

## 1. Essential storage

Essential local storage remembers whether you dismissed the cookie notice. The article section also remembers your English or Hebrew language choice. These preferences stay on your device until you clear your browser data. They are used to provide the choices you request and cannot track you across unrelated sites.

## 2. Optional analytics cookies

Google Analytics uses `_ga` and `_ga_*` cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions and measure site usage. They typically remain for up to two years, subject to configuration. Google Analytics loads when you visit the site. The site does not use advertising cookies.

## 3. Browser controls

Select "Cookie information" in the footer to reopen this notice. You can block or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings or use [Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Blocking cookies may affect saved preferences.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details about data uses, providers, transfers, and your rights. Questions can be sent to dklnsm@gmail.com.
